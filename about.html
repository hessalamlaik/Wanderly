About Us

Our Mission

Wanderly is an interest club that connects students who share a passion for travel and exploration. We make it easy to discover local and regional trips, learn from experienced activity leaders and instructors, and share travel stories with a like-minded community.

Our mission is to encourage students to step outside their comfort zone, explore new places and cultures, and build lasting friendships through shared travel experiences, while keeping every trip organised, safe and accessible to everyone.
